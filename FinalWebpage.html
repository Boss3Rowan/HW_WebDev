Moab Utah

What is Moab?

Moab, Utah, is a beautiful vacation destination known for its breathtaking red rock landscapes, stunning natural scenery, and endless outdoor adventures. Home to iconic national parks like Arches and Canyonlands, Moab offers something for everyone, from hiking, mountain biking, and off-roading to rafting and exploring unique rock formations. Whether you're looking for an exciting adventure or a relaxing getaway surrounded by nature, Moab is the perfect place to make unforgettable memories and experience some of Utah's most incredible scenery.

Fun things to do in Moab

Moab offers endless activities and adventures for visitors of all ages. You can explore the breathtaking arches and canyons, hike scenic trails, go mountain biking, ride ATVs or jeeps through the red rocks, and enjoy whitewater rafting on the Colorado River. Visitors can also go camping, rock climbing, horseback riding, stargazing, or take scenic drives to experience the beautiful desert landscape. Whether you love outdoor adventures or simply enjoying nature, Moab has something for everyone!

My Favorite Things To do in Moab

My family and I try to camp out in Moab at least once a year. We have a camp trailer that we pull down there and stay in. Sometimes we can find a good spot in our favorite camp ground but other times, like during a school break when lots of people are there, it's hard to find a good spot to park. Either way my family enjoys it there because there are really fun biking trails, one of our favorites is Slickrock (pictured below). There are different levels of ability from a crazy # mile terrain to a fun little Golden Curral with rolling hills for younger kids. Every time we go its very fun and a great vacation spot for the family.
